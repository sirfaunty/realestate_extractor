## One-liner

**Capactive turns a building's paper trail into decision-ready
intelligence — without your documents ever leaving your hands.**

## Tagline (established, use verbatim)

**See the signal. Make the move.**

## The problem (why anyone cares)

A commercial property runs on hundreds of documents — leases and their
amendments, loan agreements, rent rolls, tax bills, CAM reconciliations.
The answers owners need (What does this tenant actually owe? When does
this lease really expire? What does our lender need to see?) are buried
in scanned PDFs, and the standard options are painful: pay professionals
to abstract documents by hand, or paste confidential financials into a
cloud AI tool and hope for the best.

## What Capactive does (the story, in three beats)

1. **Read** — Capactive ingests your documents on your own computer:
   clean PDFs, rough scans, decades-old paper. AI extraction runs
   entirely locally. No document, no financial detail, ever touches a
   third-party AI service. Ever.

2. **Verify** — every extracted fact carries a citation back to the
   exact page it came from, and Capactive cross-checks documents against
   your operating data (rent rolls), flagging disagreements instead of
   papering over them. A human approves what's right before it counts.
   Nothing is invented; everything is traceable.

3. **Move** — verified data becomes working intelligence: portfolio
   dashboards, lease and loan analytics, and polished, client-ready
   documents generated in seconds — lease abstract compendiums, lender
   diligence packages — each fact still carrying its page citation.

## Key capabilities (site-friendly phrasing)

- **Private by architecture, not by promise.** Extraction runs on your
  device with a local AI model. The pitch isn't "we keep your data
  safe" — it's "your documents physically never leave."
- **Every number has a receipt.** Page-cited provenance on every
  extracted fact; one click from any figure to the source page of the
  original document.
- **Honest by design.** When the paper disagrees with the rent roll,
  Capactive flags the conflict for your judgment — it never silently
  picks a winner.
- **From filing cabinet to deliverable in seconds.** Lease abstract
  compendiums (full or executive-summary editions) and refinance
  diligence packages — loan facilities, balloon dates, rollover vs.
  maturity — generated on demand from verified data.
- **A whole team on one source of truth.** Extraction runs on licensed
  desks; everyone else — analysts, partners, lenders' counsel — works
  from dashboards and reports in the browser, with source documents one
  click away. Every change is versioned; every prior value is preserved.
- **Runs where you want it.** Your infrastructure or a private instance
  we manage for you — either way single-tenant: your instance holds your
  data and nobody else's.
- **Built for real documents.** Scanned leases from the 1990s, broken
  text layers, handwritten margins-of-error — the ingestion pipeline was
  hardened on exactly this material.

## Proof point (anonymized — do not name the client)

Validated on a real multi-state commercial portfolio: 21 properties,
80,000+ page-cited provisions, with document-to-rent-roll tie-out rates
of 92–100% per property — and every discrepancy it found was surfaced,
not smoothed over. Several of those discrepancies were real errors in
the client's existing records that Capactive caught.

## Differentiation in one sentence each

- vs. cloud AI tools (paste-into-ChatGPT et al.): they require sending
  confidential financials to someone else's servers; Capactive never does.
- vs. manual abstraction services: weeks and per-document fees become
  minutes, with citations a human can spot-check instead of redo.
- vs. generic document-management: DMs store documents; Capactive
  *understands* them and turns them into analytics and deliverables.

## Voice & style

- Tone: confident, plain-spoken, no AI hype. The buyer is skeptical of
  AI vendors — lead with control and verifiability, not "AI-powered."
- Tagline appears verbatim; don't riff variants of it.
- Observed product palette (formal brand guidelines pending): deep blue
  `#185FA5` (primary), lighter blue `#3A8FD4` (accent), dark navy/white
  wordmark ("capactive", lowercase). Ascending-bars logo mark.
- Words to prefer: verified, cited, local, yours. Words to avoid:
  revolutionary, magic, "chat with your documents."

## Claims guardrails (site must stay inside these)

- OK: "documents are processed entirely on your own device";
  "no third-party AI ever sees your documents."
- OK: "your team accesses verified results in the browser" — and if
  hosting is mentioned: single-tenant, your infrastructure or ours.
- NOT yet: mobile apps, integrations marketplace, self-serve signup,
  multi-tenant SaaS, SOC 2 (nothing certified — say "built for
  security-conscious owners," not compliance acronyms).
- Do not name the validation client or any property/tenant names.
- "AI" is fine to say; always pair it with the local/verified framing.
